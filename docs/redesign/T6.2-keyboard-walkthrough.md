## Profile drawer (`Enter` on Ibrahim's card → `#/editor/editor-label-9`)

- Focus moves to **Close**; the page behind is `inert`, the dialog is `aria-modal` and named by the Editor's name.
- `Tab`: Close → Late 29% → Speed −37% → Projects 24 → Quality → **More details** → back to Close (no stop outside the drawer);
  `Shift+Tab` goes round the other way.
- `Enter` on More details opens it; `Tab` then also reaches each finding, the four evidence links, "Open the full analysis" and the
  nested **Technical details**, and still wraps back to Close.
- `Enter` on a metric opens its evidence drawer above the profile (focus on its close button). `Escape` closes only the evidence
  drawer and returns focus to the metric; a second `Escape` closes the profile, restores the overview's scroll position and returns
  focus to Ibrahim's card.

## More details (`#/system`, or a decision card's "See the proposed rule" → `#/system/rule-quality`)

- The linked rule is scrolled into view and focused (outlined).
- The index (All decisions, Intelligence, Editors, Team context, Rules in use, Data health) is a row of buttons that jump to each part;
  the Editors list keeps its search (`/` focuses it) and filters.
